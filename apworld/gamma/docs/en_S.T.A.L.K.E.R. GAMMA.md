# S.T.A.L.K.E.R. GAMMA integration pilot

This experimental world has three first-arrival checks: Cordon, Garbage and
Rostok. Rewards are one basic toolkit, one medkit and one bandage. Visit all three
regions to complete the pilot. There are no access gates or shuffled story items
yet. It requires a fresh, isolated single-player development game.

It is an integration test, not the finished randomiser and not an xrRazom add-on.
